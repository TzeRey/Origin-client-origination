  • Company-specific vesting (including 40/30/20/10), cliffs and refresh grants
  • Prices from funding rounds, at hire and today
  • Tender offers plus federal, state and local taxes

How it works

One system. Three layers.

Data feeds intelligence. Intelligence drives action. Every conversation sharpens the next shortlist.

◈

Data

Public filings, news, secondary-market prices and professional data, unified per person and company.

  • Company filings
  • Funding-round data
  • Professional data
  • Pay benchmarks
✦

Intelligence

An equity model turns roles, tenure, vesting, tenders, taxes and prices into after-tax ranges with confidence, flags and assumptions.

  • Equity ranges
  • Vesting & tenders
  • Taxes
  • Confidence
↗

Action

Advisors filter by threshold, review the liquidity path and reach out with context, not a cold call.

  • Thresholds
  • Liquidity paths
  • Outreach
Trust

Built for regulated teams.

Private banking needs to explain every number. Origin is designed so advisors, compliance and clients can all follow the reasoning.

⌁

Source-linked

Prices, pay bands and events cite where they came from and when they were retrieved.

◎

Assumptions visible

Level bands, vesting, price range and flags are shown on every card and in a methodology panel.

✳

Advisor in the loop

Estimates prioritize conversations. They never replace client-provided information or advisor judgement.

◐

Privacy-aware

Built around professional data, with room for consent, opt-out and jurisdiction rules in your workflow.

Estimates are modeled from public data and are not verified holdings, income or net worth. Nothing here is investment, tax or legal advice. Origin does not claim third-party security certifications.
